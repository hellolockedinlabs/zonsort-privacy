Zonsort Privacy Policy

Locked In Labs · Effective September 25, 2026

In short: Zonsort does not ask for your name, email, location or any other personal information, and we (Locked In Labs) do not collect it. Your game progress is saved on your device. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

This policy explains how the Zonsort app for Android ("Zonsort", "the game") handles information. It applies to Zonsort published on Google Play by Locked In Labs ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information. Zonsort has no accounts, no sign-up, no chat and no way for players to share information with each other. We do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress and settings: levels completed, stars, best move counts, coins, and your music, sound and vibration settings. This is saved only on your device.
  • A random save identifier: the game creates a random code on your device so progress from the same device can be combined correctly. It is not linked to you, your device's hardware IDs or your Google account, and it is never sent to us.
  • Android backup: if you have turned on Android backup on your device, Android may copy the game's save file to your own Google account so your progress can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by Google and your device settings, and we cannot see these backups. Google's privacy policy applies.

In-app purchases

Zonsort offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase was made so it can be unlocked and restored on your devices. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy.

Internet access

The game uses the internet only to talk to Google Play for purchases. You can play all levels offline.

Advertising

The current version of Zonsort does not show ads. If we add ads in a future version, they will be limited to ads suitable for children, will not be personalized or based on interests, and will be served only through ad services approved under Google Play's Families policy. We will update this policy before that change.

Children's privacy

Zonsort is designed to be suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The game has no features that let children share personal information. Purchases are made through Google Play, which provides parental controls such as purchase approval through Google Family Link. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling Zonsort or clearing the app's storage in your Android settings. Android backups can be managed in your Google account settings.

Security

Since we do not collect personal information, none of it is held on our servers. Purchase information is handled securely by Google Play.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Zonsort handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will be made before they take effect in the game.
